2 Dot Product

Dot Product

a⋅b=∣a∣∣b∣cos⁡θ\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \vert{}\mathbf{a}\vert{}\vert{}\mathbf{b}\vert{}\cos\thetaa⋅b=∣a∣∣b∣cosθ

計出嚟嘅結果: 數字 Scalar

如果有a=(a1,a2,a3)\mathbf{a}=(a_1,a_2,a_3)a=(a1​,a2​,a3​), b=(b1,b2,b3)\mathbf{b}=(b_1,b_2,b_3)b=(b1​,b2​,b3​)：

a⋅b=a1b1+a2b2+a3b3\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = a_1 b_1 + a_2 b_2 + a_3 b_3a⋅b=a1​b1​+a2​b2​+a3​b3​

Dot Product = 0 即垂直，垂直則 Dot Product = 0

a⋅b=0  ⟺  a⊥b\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 0 \iff \mathbf{a} \perp \mathbf{b}a⋅b=0⟺a⊥b

用 dot product 計長度

自己 dot 自己等於長度 2 次：

a⋅a=∣a∣2\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{a} = \vert{}\mathbf{a}\vert{}^2a⋅a=∣a∣2

∣a∣=a⋅a\vert{}\mathbf{a}\vert{}= \sqrt{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{a}}∣a∣=a⋅a​

Example

There are two vectors a\mathbf{a}a and b\mathbf{b}b, where ∣a∣=3\vert{}\mathbf{a}\vert{} = 3∣a∣=3, ∣b∣=5\vert{}\mathbf{b}\vert{} = 5∣b∣=5, and the angle

between the two vectors is 2π3\dfrac{2\pi}{3}32π​.


(a) Find a⋅b\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}a⋅b.

(b) Let c\mathbf{c}c be a vector such that a+b+c=0\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c} = \mathbf{0}a+b+c=0. Find ∣c∣\vert{}\mathbf{c}\vert{}∣c∣.


Solution

(a)

a⋅b=∣a∣∣b∣cos⁡θ=(3)(5)cos⁡(2π3)=15(−12)=−152\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \vert{}\mathbf{a}\vert{}\vert{}\mathbf{b}\vert{}\cos\theta = (3)(5)\cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 15\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = -\frac{15}{2}a⋅b=∣a∣∣b∣cosθ=(3)(5)cos(32π​)=15(−21​)=−215​

(b)

From a+b+c=0\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c} = \mathbf{0}a+b+c=0, we have c=−(a+b)\mathbf{c} = -(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b})c=−(a+b).

∣c∣2=−(a+b)⋅−(a+b)=∣a∣2+2(a⋅b)+∣b∣2\vert{}\mathbf{c}\vert{}^2 = -(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) \cdot -(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) = \vert{}\mathbf{a}\vert{}^2 + 2(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}) + \vert{}\mathbf{b}\vert{}^2∣c∣2=−(a+b)⋅−(a+b)=∣a∣2+2(a⋅b)+∣b∣2

Substituting the known values:

∣c∣2=32+2(−152)+52=19\vert{}\mathbf{c}\vert{}^2 = 3^2 + 2\left(-\frac{15}{2}\right) + 5^2 = 19∣c∣2=32+2(−215​)+52=19

∣c∣=19\vert{}\mathbf{c}\vert{} = \sqrt{19}∣c∣=19​
